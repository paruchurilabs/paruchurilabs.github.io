How the price is figured

  • Each load is priced at your rate for that share of the truck, from a 1/8 minimum load up to a full truck.
  • Mattress disposal is priced per piece, and other dump or recycling fees are passed through as entered.
  • Heavy debris, stairs, same-day service and hot tubs are flat add-ons.
  • If the total falls under your minimum, the minimum applies.

Common questions

How much should I charge for junk removal?

Most junk removal companies price by how much of the truck a job fills. Published guides put a 1/8 or minimum load around $100 to $250, a half truck around $300 to $500 and a full truck around $600 to $800, with national franchises charging up to about $1,100 for a full load.

How do you price junk removal by the truckload?

Estimate by eye how much of the truck bed the items will take up, then price from a load chart: 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 3/4 and full. Share the chart up front and confirm the final price once the items are loaded, so the customer only pays for the space they use.

What is a good minimum charge for junk removal?

Many companies set a minimum pickup between $70 and $150, which covers a single bulky item or a very small load. Single-item pickups such as a chair, mattress or appliance commonly run $70 to $180.

Should I charge extra for mattresses, heavy debris or stairs?

Yes. Transfer stations often charge $20 to $50 to take a mattress, so many haulers pass that fee through. Heavy debris like concrete or dirt commonly adds $100 to $300 or more, and stairs or long carries often add $50 to $150.
