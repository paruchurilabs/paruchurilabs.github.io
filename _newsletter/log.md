# Booked & Busy log

Weekly email for home service business owners (house cleaning, lawn care, pressure washing, window cleaning, and the trades of any Field Quote Kit calculator), mostly owner-operators and teams of 1-5, written by Krish Paruchuri. Broadened from cleaning-only on 2026-10-17 so every calculator page feeds one list. Write tips that apply across trades, using examples from different trades; an issue may focus on one trade now and then. Sent Tuesday mornings on beehiiv. Under 3 minutes to read.

Format, every issue: (1) The tip: one practical lesson, ~150 words. (2) Copy-paste this: a ready-to-use text, email or price line. (3) Tool of the week: one tool with an honest take on who it's for (affiliate slot once links are approved; Field Quote Kit tools count too). (4) One-line sign-off, signed "Krish", with a soft link to a calculator.
