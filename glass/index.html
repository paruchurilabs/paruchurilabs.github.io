Stained Glass Sign Personalizer

Add your names and date, check the preview, then print your full-size pattern. Everything happens in your browser, and nothing is uploaded or saved.

Full size: choose "Save as PDF" in the print window, then take the PDF to a print shop (an engineering or blueprint print is cheapest). Set scale to 100% / Actual size.
Tiles: prints on regular letter paper at home. Trim along the marks and tape the pages together in order (A1, A2, ...).
