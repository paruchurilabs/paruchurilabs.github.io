Our story

Three generations, one pot of broth

Lan opened this kitchen in 2011 with her daughter Mai, cooking the phở she learned from her mother in Saigon. Today Mai's son Kevin runs the front of house, and Lan still tastes every pot before the doors open.

We make everything in-house: the broth, the pickled carrots and daikon, the lemongrass pork, and the chili oil people keep asking to buy by the jar.

How the broth is made
  1. 5:00amBeef bones are blanched, rinsed and set to simmer.
  2. 6:30amCharred onion and ginger go in, with star anise, cinnamon and clove.
  3. 11:00amFirst bowls served. The pot keeps simmering all day.
  4. 5:00pmTwelve hours in: the richest broth of the day for dinner.
