Questions

Common questions

Do I need to provide supplies?

No. We bring everything, including vacuums and mops. If you prefer a specific product, leave it out and we'll use it.

Do I have to be home?

No. Most clients leave a key, lockbox or door code. We'll text you when we arrive and when we leave.

What if I need to reschedule?

Just let us know 48 hours ahead and there's no charge.

How do I pay?

Card, Zelle or Venmo after each clean. No deposits and no contracts; recurring service can be paused anytime.
