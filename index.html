Questions

Common questions

Do I need to know anything about computers?

No. You tell me what you want, and I take care of everything technical.

Who owns the website?

You own your web address. If you ever cancel the care plan, I'll help you move the site wherever you like.

What do I need to get started?

Your business name, phone, hours, a list of services or menu with prices, and a few photos. Phone photos are fine.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. The launch is a one-time payment and the care plan is month to month.

How do I pay?

By card or bank transfer through an invoice I email you. Half to start, half when the site goes live.
